Design three mobile screens for "MindPlan", an AI meditation prep tool designed for American millennials (born ~1985-1996). The aesthetic is warm, editorial, analog-meets-digital, grounded, and personal — like a quiet corner in a sunlit room.

Screen 1: "The Inquiry" — where an AI asks deep questions to build a personalized meditation.
Screen 2: "The Meditation" — immersive full-screen audio meditation player.
Screen 3: "My Space" — a minimal, calm settings and preferences area.

Global style:
- Zero bottom tabs, zero navigation bars. The app is a single-purpose sanctuary.
- Palette: warm oatmeal (#F3EDE4), dusty terracotta (#C27B6C), muted sage (#8FA88A), faded denim blue (#7B93A8), soft sand (#E8DFD3), deep warm charcoal (#3C3835). No pure white, no pure black.
- Texture: very subtle paper grain (~3% opacity) across all backgrounds.
- Typography: warm, slightly rounded sans-serif (Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans) for headers and labels; relaxed handwritten style (Caveat) for user input.
- Motion: breath-paced, fluid, "weighted" like a smooth pebble dropping into still water.
- Frosted elements feel like matte sea glass, not slick tech blur.
- Soft, diffuse shadows only where necessary. No harsh borders.

**Screen 1: The Inquiry — "Building Your Meditation"**

*Frame:* iPhone 15 Pro (390x844).

*Background:* Warm oatmeal (#F3EDE4) with faint paper grain. Very slow shifting of warm tones — oatmeal to soft sand — creating gentle aliveness. Faint, widely-spaced matte gold motes drift upward like dust in afternoon light.

*Top-right corner access to "My Space":* A barely-there icon — three tiny, uneven matte dots in warm grey — sits in the top right. Tapping it gently transitions to Screen 3 (My Space).

*The Building Ring:*
Centered vertically at ~40% from top. A large, elegant ring — thin, slightly textured, brushed matte gold, very slightly irregular (hand-drawn feel). Each completed Q&A round fills one segment with a warm, earthy terracotta-gold arc with a subtle matte glow. A tiny step counter ("4 of 10") fades in at the ring's lower curve with each question in subtle warm grey serif, then fades after 2 seconds.

*AI Question:*
Inside the ring, in deep warm charcoal (#3C3835), 20-22px, rounded sans-serif with generous line spacing. Feels like a thoughtful friend. Example: "Where in your body do you feel that scattered energy living right now?"

*Input Area:*
Below the ring (~65% from top), a soft rounded pill (290×48px) of frosted sea glass — matte, slightly textured, warm oatmeal tone with subtle edge shadows. Inside:

- **Left — Voice Dot:** Matte terracotta dot (16px) with a slow amber halo that breathes. When held, the halo expands into a softly pulsing ring. Pulse speeds up slightly when speech is detected — a quiet, organic acknowledgment. If held without speech, nothing happens; the ring stays patient, then contracts on release. No error, no prompt. Calm patience.

- **Right — Text Line:** A muted, earthy gold line (like aged brass) that extends as the user types. Text appears in relaxed handwritten font, warm brownish-charcoal. Keyboard slides up naturally; the line and pill remain visible. Cursor is a tiny, slow-blinking matte dot.

- **Submit Trigger — "The Glow":** At the far right of the text line, when text exists, a small matte gold glyph fades in: a tiny, slightly imperfect circle with a soft upward-curling tail — like a breath mark or wisp of incense smoke. Glows gently, patiently. Tapping it submits. Invisible when line is empty.

*Submission with Undo:*
On tap, a warm ripple travels left along the line. The text lifts and dissolves into the Building Ring as the next segment fills with a soft terracotta arc. A delicate "Undo" text in subtle grey serif fades in at the bottom for 3 seconds. Tapping restores the text. After 3 seconds, it fades away.

*Character Counter & Quick-Start:*
Lower right, five tiny matte dots like dried petals or pebbles in muted earth tones. They light up at 20/40/60/80/100 characters. When all five glow, the Building Ring pulses warmly, and the submit glyph transforms into a more complete circle with a tiny ascending spark — inviting a jump to meditation. User can ignore or tap. Full agency.

*No Chat History:*
Only the current question and in-progress input exist. The Building Ring is the only record — visual, wordless progress.

**Screen 2: The Meditation — "Your Meditation"**

*Frame:* 390x844.

*Background:* Deep muted indigo with subtle warm undertones — like desert sky just after last light. Same subtle grain texture. Sparse, slow-drifting motes in soft warm white, like distant stars or settling dust.

*The Halo:*
The completed ring from inquiry, now a full, softly breathing halo — matte terracotta-gold, warm and grounded. Pulses at resting breath rhythm. Inside, personalized meditation title in small warm cream sans-serif (16px, light weight), e.g. "Space for What's Scattered."

*Timer:*
The halo itself is the timer. A subtle, warm-toned section dims clockwise — a gentle darkening, like a slow shadow. Below the halo, remaining time in understated lightweight text: "10:12".

*Final Minute Cue:*
In the last 60 seconds, the halo's pulse deepens slightly — a gentle, wordless nudge.

*Controls:*
Completely hidden. A single tap brings up a frosted sea-glass pill at the bottom with a subtle pause icon (two soft, rounded vertical bars) and an end icon (small, incomplete circle). The pill fades after 3 seconds.

*Breath Cues:*
Occasionally, a very soft, warm ring of light expands outward from the halo, syncing with an exhale.

*End State:*
Halo contracts back into thin ring outline. Message in warm understated type: "Whenever you're ready, we can build another." Outline stays, inviting a tap to restart. After 10 seconds of no interaction, screen fades back to inquiry.

**Screen 3: My Space — "Your Corner"**

*Frame:* 390x844.

*Background:* Same warm oatmeal (#F3EDE4) with subtle paper grain as Screen 1, creating continuity. The same slow, barely-perceptible warm tone shifting. Very few, widely-spaced matte gold motes drift even more slowly than on Screen 1 — like the room has settled into stillness.

*Header:*
At the top, centered, the word "Your Space" in small, warm charcoal serif (~14px, letter-spaced), understated. No back arrow icon — instead, a small, subtle "return" glyph in the top-left corner: a tiny, matte gold circle with a soft leftward tail, like a reversed breath mark. Tapping it transitions smoothly back to Screen 1.

*Content layout:*
Below the header, the settings appear as a series of gently separated, frosted sea-glass cards — each a soft, rounded rectangle (matte, slightly textured, warm oatmeal with subtle edge shadow). Cards are spaced with generous breathing room. No harsh dividers, no toggle switches that snap — everything is soft and organic.

**Card 1 — "Default Duration":**
A single row. Left: "Default duration" in warm charcoal sans-serif (~16px). Right: a horizontal row of four subtle, rounded pills, each with a time option: "5 min" / "10 min" / "15 min" / "20 min". The currently selected pill is filled with a soft, matte terracotta glow; unselected pills are outlined in muted warm grey. Tapping a pill animates a gentle fill transition — like warm watercolor blooming on paper. This duration becomes the default for new inquiries (can be overridden during Q&A when the AI asks about time).

**Card 2 — "Voice Style":**
A single row. Left: "Voice style" in warm charcoal sans-serif. Right: two soft rounded pills side by side: "Warm" and "Calm". One is selected with the same matte terracotta fill. These are subtle tonal preferences for the AI-generated meditation narration — "Warm" is slightly more personal and conversational, "Calm" is more spacious and neutral.

**Card 3 — "Background Sound":**
Left: "Background sound" in warm charcoal sans-serif. Right: a horizontal scrollable row of tiny, elegant circular icons, each representing a soundscape option: a leaf (forest), a wave (ocean), a flame (crackling fire), a drop (rain), a circle (silence/none). The selected icon has a soft terracotta glow around its edge. Tapping an icon gives a subtle pulse and selects it. This becomes the default background sound for generated meditations.

**Card 4 — "Input Preference":**
Left: "I prefer to…" in warm charcoal sans-serif. Right: two soft rounded pills: "Speak" and "Type". The selected one has the matte terracotta fill. This sets the initial input focus when the inquiry screen opens — if "Speak" is selected, the voice dot has a subtle initial glow to gently invite speaking first; if "Type," the text line cursor blinks softly on arrival. Users can always use both methods regardless of preference.

**Card 5 — "About":**
At the bottom, with extra breathing room above, a single understated row: "About MindPlan" in muted warm grey, with a tiny forward-facing chevron-like mark (soft and rounded, not sharp). Tapping reveals a minimal, elegantly typeset paragraph about the app's philosophy — "MindPlan builds a meditation just for you, right now, through gentle questions. Nothing is stored. Nothing follows you. Each session is a fresh beginning." This text lives on the same card, expanding it with a smooth animation.

*Reset Option:*
At the very bottom of the screen, in small, muted warm grey text, the word "Reset all preferences" with a tiny, subtle circular arrow glyph. Tapping it triggers a gentle confirmation: the text changes to "Tap again to confirm" for 3 seconds. If tapped again, all settings return to defaults with a soft, warm dissolve-and-reappear animation. If not tapped again, it reverts to the original text.

*No Account, No Data, No History:*
There is no login, no profile picture, no stats, no streak counts, no meditation history, no connected apps, no notifications toggle. The screen is clean, personal, and private — a quiet corner to adjust only what matters.

---

*Transitions between all three screens:*
- Screen 1 ↔ Screen 3: A gentle crossfade with a subtle "breath" contraction/expansion of the background gradient (~0.6s).
- Screen 1 → Screen 2: The Building Ring expands into full-screen warm glow, then dims into the meditation backdrop (as described).
- Screen 2 → Screen 1: On meditation end or user tap to return, the halo contracts back into the Building Ring outline. A smooth crossfade returns to the inquiry screen.

---

**Output:** Three high-fidelity mobile UI mockups, side by side. Screen 1 shows the Building Ring partially filled, the sea-glass input pill with voice dot, text line, submit glyph, and the counter dots. Screen 2 shows the full breathing halo in deep indigo with timer. Screen 3 shows "Your Space" with the frosted settings cards, one duration pill selected, and the overall calm, editorial layout. Everything communicates warmth, intentionality, and quiet personal space — no chat app DNA, no social features, no data clutter.
