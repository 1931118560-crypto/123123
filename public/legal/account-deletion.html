MindPlan Account Deletion

Operator: Shenzhen Shuyuxinyuan Technology Co., Ltd.

How to Delete in App

  1. Open MindPlan.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find Delete Account & Data.
  4. Tap once to start, then tap again to confirm.

Deletion removes your account and related app data, including interaction history, telemetry records linked to your account/device, and entitlement records used by the app.
