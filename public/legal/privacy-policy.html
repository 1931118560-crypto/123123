MindPlan Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindPlan, operated by Shenzhen Shuyuxinyuan Technology Co., Ltd. ("MindPlan", "we", "our", "us"), collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our app and related services.

Summary:
  • We process your meditation interaction data to provide core AI functionality.
  • You can disable interaction-content upload in the app at My Space → Legal & Privacy.
  • You can request deletion directly in the app using Delete Account & Data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account/session identifiers generated by our authentication system.
  • Device identifiers and app diagnostics (for reliability and anti-abuse).
  • Meditation interaction content (such as prompts, responses, generated scripts) when upload is enabled.
  • Subscription and entitlement records for billing and access control.

2. How We Use Information

  • Deliver core app features, including personalized meditation generation.
  • Maintain service security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Operate subscriptions, purchase restoration, and entitlement checks.
  • Improve product quality and user experience.

3. Your Controls

  • In-app upload toggle: You may disable interaction-content upload at any time.
  • Account/data deletion: You may delete your account and associated data in-app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers that support hosting, authentication, payments, and analytics, under appropriate contractual protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. If you delete your account in-app, we delete relevant data linked to your account/device, subject to legal or security exceptions.

6. Children

MindPlan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. International Use

If you use MindPlan outside the United States, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions where our providers operate.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at yidilisiahati@shuyuxinyuan.cn.
