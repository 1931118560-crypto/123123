Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026. MindPlan collects only the data needed to provide AI meditation functionality, secure the service, and manage subscription entitlements.

  • Collected data may include account/session identifiers, device diagnostics, and meditation interaction data.
  • Users can disable interaction-content upload in app settings.
  • Users can request account and data deletion directly in the app.
  • MindPlan does not sell personal information.

Terms of Service

MindPlan provides AI-assisted mindfulness experiences for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not medical advice.

  • Users must use the service lawfully and responsibly.
  • Subscriptions are billed via Apple ID and renew automatically unless canceled in advance.
  • Users can manage subscriptions at Apple Subscriptions.

Subscription Terms

Current offer: 7-day free trial, then USD $7.99/month (unless otherwise shown in-app).

Renewal is automatic. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

Account Deletion

To delete your account in the app:

  • Open MindPlan and go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account & Data.
  • Tap again to confirm permanent deletion.

If app access is unavailable, email yidilisiahati@shuyuxinyuan.cn for manual assistance.
